How we design and build your website

  1. Discover the business. Tell us what you sell, who the website is for and the action you want visitors to take. Share your current site, logo, copy, photographs and design references.
  2. Agree the structure and scope. We outline the pages, content, interactions and integrations. You can see what is included in the quote and what needs a separate decision.
  3. Design and build. We translate the approved direction into responsive layouts, with readable type, useful navigation and a consistent visual system.
  4. Test and launch. We check mobile layouts, links and enquiry paths, add the agreed search metadata and confirm hosting and domain responsibilities before launch.

Optional AI agents, chatbots and automations are scoped around a specific task, such as answering FAQs or routing an enquiry. An assistant is useful only when its knowledge, limits and human handoff are clear.

Choosing a website designer in Pakistan

When comparing agencies or freelancers, ask to see relevant work and how it behaves on a normal phone. Check what the proposal includes: pages, content, forms, revisions, hosting, domain ownership and support. Ask how a visitor will contact you and who will test that flow. A dramatic homepage is useful only if the services and next step remain clear.

For a quote from Brandier Studio, send your page list, reference websites, required features and target launch date. We will discuss the scope rather than attach an unverified promise of traffic, leads or first-page rankings to the design.

Questions before we start

How much does a business website cost?

The quote depends on the page count, content, integrations, motion and support you need. Send a short brief and reference websites for a scoped proposal. Our advertising packages are separate from custom website quotes.

Do you work only with businesses in Karachi?

No. Brandier Studio is based in Karachi and works remotely with businesses across Pakistan and internationally.

Will my website work on mobile?

Responsive layouts and mobile navigation are part of the website design approach. The agreed project scope defines the devices, browsers and enquiry flows we test.

Can you add 3D animation, a chatbot or automation?

Yes, these can be scoped as part of the project or as separate work. We first agree the interaction, the required assets or tools, and how it should work for your visitors.

Can you guarantee first-page rankings?

No. Search visibility depends on content, competition, website quality and many other factors. We can implement the agreed SEO foundations, but cannot promise a particular Google position.
